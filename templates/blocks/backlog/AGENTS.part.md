## Backlog: future work items in order

Optional. `work/BACKLOG.md` holds future work items, one line each, in the order they will be done; the next
item is the first line whose `after:` items are all archived. The owner changes the order by moving lines; a
line is deleted when its item gets a sheet. Before archiving an item, every open `Owner TODO` line and
everything it deferred (an `X-NN`, a `gaps` line, a question moved out) becomes a backlog line, or a line in
the sheet of the item it belongs to; none stays only in the archive. A line is one line; detail waits for the
item's sheet.
